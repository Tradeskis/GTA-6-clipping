**Last updated: 18 September 2026**

## Scope

These terms cover a private, single-operator tool that turns gameplay footage
recorded by its owner into short vertical videos and uploads them to the
owner's own YouTube channel.

The tool is not distributed and is not offered to anyone else. Its only user is
its owner, David Knapp, operating the channel
[GTA 6 Viral Moments](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCTyGxG-QYWMuPqSobydMxog).

There is no public sign-up, no account system, and no service provided to third
parties. These terms exist to state how the tool is operated.

## Source material

The tool processes only footage that its operator has recorded himself or is
otherwise licensed to use.

It does not download, scrape or otherwise acquire video from other creators,
from YouTube, or from any other platform. Every file it processes is supplied
deliberately by the operator from local storage.

## What the tool does

1. Reads a gameplay recording from local storage.
2. Transcribes its speech and detects notable moments in its audio and video.
3. Selects short segments and writes titles and descriptions for them.
4. Renders each segment as a vertical video with captions.
5. Uploads the result to the operator's own channel as a private video and
   sets a scheduled publication time.
6. Reads back view counts for the operator's own published videos.

## What the tool does not do

It does not, and will not:

- inflate views, likes, subscribers or any other metric
- post comments, send messages, or interact with other users or channels
- access, read or modify any channel other than its operator's own
- circumvent, disable or interfere with any part of the YouTube API or service
- resell, redistribute or provide access to YouTube data
- cache YouTube data beyond what is needed for the operations above

## Accuracy of generated text

Titles, descriptions and on-screen captions are generated automatically from
what is actually present in the footage. The tool is built to refuse claims its
source material does not support, and the operator reviews published material
for accuracy.
